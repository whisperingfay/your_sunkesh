------------- E-COMMERCE SYSTEMS -------------

STEP 1: Create a CATEGORY (Optional but recommended)
URL: http://localhost/mysenkesh/your_sunkesh/api/v1/categories.php?name=Electronics
Purpose: Organize products


STEP 2: Create PRODUCTS (under a category)
URL: http://localhost/mysenkesh/your_sunkesh/api/v1/products.php?name=AirPods&price=1500
Purpose: Add items for sale


STEP 3: Create INVENTORY for the Product
URL: http://localhost/mysenkesh/your_sunkesh/api/v1/inventory.php?product_id=1&stock=100
Purpose: Track stock levels or availability


STEP 4: Create a CUSTOMER
URL: http://localhost/mysenkesh/your_sunkesh/api/v1/customers.php?name=Juan&email=juan@email.com
Purpose: Register buyer or Customer


STEP 5: Create an ORDER (Customer buys Product)
URL: http://localhost/mysenkesh/your_sunkesh/api/v1/orders.php?customer_id=1&product_id=1&quantity=2
Purpose: Record purchase


STEP 6: Create PAYMENT for the ORDER
URL: http://localhost/mysenkesh/your_sunkesh/api/v1/payments.php?order_id=1&amount=3000&method=gcash
Purpose: Record payment


STEP 7: Update INVENTORY after Order
URL: http://localhost/mysenkesh/your_sunkesh/api/v1/inventory.php?update=1&id=1&stock=98
Purpose: Reduce stock because 2 units were sold


STEP 8: Create REVIEW (Customer reviews the Product)
URL: http://localhost/mysenkesh/your_sunkesh/api/v1/reviews.php?product_id=1&customer_id=1&rating=5&comment=Excellent%20product!
Purpose: Customer feedback
